# Creator and AR scan test guide

Use the updated tagtag app on the iPhone 15 Pro Max. Allow camera and Precise Location, and sign in for saving or publishing.

## Create

1. Open STICK → sticker grid → Add sticker. Expect three illustrated choices: **Upload**, **Photo**, **Imagine**, with no overflowing labels.
2. Upload a photo or transparent PNG, edit it, and save. Expect to return to the creator. Open **My designs** to see the saved sticker in Home.
3. Try **Photo** with the camera and photo library. Expect the existing Polaroid editor. Cancel once and reopen.
4. Try **Imagine** where Apple Intelligence is available. Otherwise expect a short unavailable message and usable alternatives.
   Generate an image, tap Done, and confirm the image opens in the sticker editor. Also cancel and reopen Image Playground.
5. Repeat with larger system text. Check tile labels and scrolling. Back should return to the three-column sticker grid.

## Place and scan

1. Select a sticker and point at a textured, well-lit floor or wall. Expect **Find surface**, then **Place sticker** when a surface is detected.
2. Check that the detected area has translucent yellow pencil strokes. The camera image should remain visible, and strokes should stop at the detected boundary.
3. Tap the area, drag, pinch, and rotate the sticker. Expect **Scan surroundings**, then **Scan ready** when AR mapping is ready. Move slowly around the placement if needed.
   The camera and Write note should explain scanning from several angles. **Continue scanning** should close the note sheet while keeping your text.
4. Cover the camera or briefly background the app. Ready must withdraw when tracking is lost; recover by moving slowly. Open and close a sheet and confirm the scan state does not reset just because the surface fill is hidden.

## Note and publish

1. Open **Write note** after placement. It must not show a design-library “Route not found” error.
2. Enter the note, teaser, and place; publish after scan and location requirements are met. Record any disabled-button guidance and elapsed time from tapping Publish to completion.
3. Create a second sticker and confirm it appears in My designs after saving. If saving fails, the creator should show an error and retry option; your note should remain intact.

## Shared preview and sheets

1. Publish a new sticker with the sticker and its surroundings visible to the camera. On another phone running this updated app, start finding it nearby.
2. Expect an **Original spot** thumbnail above the camera controls. Tap it to enlarge the saved scene; close it and compare with the live camera. Viewing the photo must not unlock the note or count as finding the sticker.
3. Older stickers should show **No reference photo** and remain discoverable. Both phones need this update to read new photo-bearing AR maps.
4. Open the inventory, creator, Write note, and photo sheets. Their paper background should reach the bottom edge without a transparent gap. Content should stay above the home indicator. Open the keyboard and check that note fields remain reachable.

Report the step, screenshot, approximate time, and whether the problem survives reopening the app. Native Apple Intelligence, physical AR tracking, and end-to-end publishing require these device checks; editor captures do not verify them.
