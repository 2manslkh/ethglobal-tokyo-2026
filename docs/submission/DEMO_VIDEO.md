# Demo video outline

The ETHGlobal Video step labels an upload optional, but the project-creation guidelines say a video is required for finalist prizes. Use a 2–4 minute `.mp4` or `.mov` at 720p or higher with clear spoken audio. Do not speed up footage or use synthetic narration. [Official guidance](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo2026/info/details).

| Time | Visual | Spoken point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Login, Home, Taggi | “tagtag lets people leave a little discovery at a real place and collect discoveries left by others.” |
| 0:20–1:15 | Choose artwork, scan a real surface, place, write note, publish | Show the real creator flow. Explain that the note is private until collection. |
| 1:15–2:15 | Second iPhone: Explore pin, teaser, reference photo, Find in AR, recovered sticker | Show the same physical spot and the saved AR placement. If this has not passed on a device, describe it as a prototype and show only verified behavior. |
| 2:15–2:50 | Tap, celebration, book, revealed note | Show the result only if the real collection succeeds. |
| 2:50–3:20 | Brief architecture/source view | Unity/ARKit, MapKit, Firebase, Cloud Run. State clearly that Sepolia souvenir minting is disabled pending verification. |
| 3:20–3:40 | Closing screen | Explain what was built during the event and direct judges to the public repo. |

Record the screen from the device or simulator and assemble it on a computer; ETHGlobal's guidance says not to film the submission with a phone camera. Keep unedited capture segments and the final export. A current, honest demo of the verified one-phone publishing flow is preferable to staging an unverified finder journey.
