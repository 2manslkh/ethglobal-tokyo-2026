# tagtag pitch deck

Audience: ETHGlobal judges. Nine slides, including one dedicated DEMO slide. Timing and exact narration: [VIDEO_SCRIPT.md](VIDEO_SCRIPT.md).

## Visual direction

Use the app’s paper white and warm paper, charcoal ink, restrained yellow, Shadows Into Light headings, and Instrument Sans body text. Use existing Taggi illustrations at their natural proportions. Large readable type, generous space, and one main idea per slide. Actual app captures remain labelled as simulator/UI previews. Editable text throughout.
