# tagtag: four-minute pitch

Nine slides. Total running time **4:00**, including **1:15 for the live demo** on slide 6. Advance at the timestamps below. Rehearse with a timer; the demo contains deliberate pauses for interaction.

## 0:00–0:15 · Slide 1 · tagtag

“What if your favourite places had something waiting for you? tagtag is an augmented reality collecting game that helps communities create discoveries worth sharing. Find your places. Collect your moments.”

## 0:15–0:45 · Slide 2 · Two inspirations

“POAP showed how a digital collectible can carry the memory of being there. Reports put its lifetime reach at nearly 7.6 million badges, created by more than 46,000 issuers.

Pokémon GO showed the joy of taking a collecting game into the real world. It gave people a reason to explore and meet in shared places.

tagtag brings those ideas together: discovery with something personal to keep.”

## 0:45–1:05 · Slide 3 · A place has a story

“Think about a café you love, a corner of your campus, or a conversation at a hackathon. Each could hold a small discovery for the next person.

We want to give communities a playful way to share those places, with a keepsake that remembers the experience.”

## 1:05–1:30 · Slide 4 · The collecting loop

“A creator chooses a sticker, places it on a real surface in AR, and leaves a clue and a note.

An explorer follows a nearby map pin and looks for the sticker at that spot. Collecting adds a copy to their sticker book and reveals the note. The original stays there for someone else to discover.”

## 1:30–1:55 · Slide 5 · A game communities can make their own

“Imagine a conference where each community leaves a sticker at its gathering place. Or a neighbourhood walk curated by local artists.

The community supplies the stories and the places. Players explore, collect, and share what they found. Those shared discoveries give people a reason to meet and a reason to return. These are the experiences we want to test next.”

## 3:10–3:35 · Slide 7 · How it works

“We built the iPhone experience in Unity with ARKit, and use Apple’s maps for nearby discovery. Firebase and our Cloud Run API store stickers, collections, and private notes.

We have also implemented on chain souvenirs, bringing the idea of digital keepsakes into tagtag. The collecting experience works without a blockchain transaction.”

## 3:35–3:50 · Slide 8 · The first community pilot

“Next, we want to run a small community trail: a handful of meaningful places, a set of original stickers, and real people trying the full journey. We’ll learn whether they finish the trail and come back for another.”

## 3:50–4:00 · Slide 9 · Find your places. Collect your moments.

“tagtag turns places into invitations, and discoveries into keepsakes. We’re looking for a community to create the first trail with us. Thank you.”
